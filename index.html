WELCOME

To Deicidium, an official home-made wiki created by idi0cy. A simple and (hopefully) efficient method of navigating through all canonical/official content of Deicidium: A multi-disciplinary personal project.

DEICIDIUM

Deicide meaning "the killing of a god," or "one who kills a god," stems from the Latin combination of deus (god), and the suffix -cidium (to cut or kill): Deicidium

ABOUT

Deicidium originated as a personal writing project that has extended into other areas (web development, for one). It is a high fantasy narrative which follows the journeys of a diverse cast of characters through the multiverse spaced out over centuries.

In the (proclaimed) first year, a prophet of God(?) named the Akrilith descended unto the mortal plane to deliver a message.

"I am the voice, will, and soul of God. Something something something, I haven't finished retconning all this yet."

The Akrilith is founded off expansion of God's grace. This expansion can and often will come at the cost of all those in its path. Only your time can tell whether it can be stopped.

THE LORE

Deicidium truly started when I decided to worldbuild random things in my backyard to burn time during the summer. Those ideas sat idly by until my school established "silent writing blocks," where I actually decided to consolidate some of that.

Naturally, the writing project was soon abandoned when I moved on to other hobbies. Now in the big 26, I'm bringing it back because I still think it sounds cool, and I have unfinished business with the plotline.

With the power of hindsight, I now realize that everything to do with this project needs some major work. While I rewrite all the worldbuilding pieces and story elements, I'm going to do some parallel processing and work on this website.
