NOOSPHERE
"Mind before matter: reality made thinking"

The noosphere is a metaphysical (not traditionally existent) "space" that we can start by describing as the "plane of ideas." This is not an inaccurate description, however there is more to it than that which will be discussed later on.

All things within the noosphere are ideological in nature. This means that the first fundamental aspect we need to acknowledge are ideas themselves. What is an idea? What are they made of? To answer that, let's discuss where these ideas are sitting around in. You can imagine it as a very complex spider web, infinitely complex in fact. Each idea is a region on this metaphorical web. If one region of this web was the concept of death, it would be able to contain other ideas; for example, within death would be skeletons, or zombies. Those are both dead, so they are within the wider concept of death. However, some concepts (like zombies) will not fully belong in the concept of death. Similarly to a Venn diagram, concepts exist in the overlap.

Where does an idea begin and where does one end? If I keep flattening a bowl more and more, at which point does it become a plate? This is thinking and questioning unfit for ideological boundaries, as they do not have any. Ideas don't have borders, they act in gradients. There is no fine line between dead and alive, or real and imaginary. The concept of life is a gradient (or spectrum); the closer you get to it, the closer you are to the concept of life. On the contrary, when you start to leave the gradient, the less "living" you are considered to be. A human, in traditional thinking, has a much higher concentration of the living concept than a zombie.

Five blind men are trying to figure out what the elephant in front of them is (original, I know. Don't mention it.) One believes it to be a tree trunk, feeling its leg. Someone else believes it to be a spear, feeling it's tusk. The other three... well they think something, but you get the point. Let's discuss the conscious and the subjective noosphere.

At the point, the obvious question is posed. If the concept of life is supposed to be one region in the noosphere, why do all things view it as different? I may not believe an artificial intelligence is alive (please don't murder me, tech overlords), but someone in the future with differing morals might believe it is. Fear not, because there is no singular concept for "life" amongst all thinkers. Similarly to how we view country borders and count continents, the noosphere is interpretable in an unlimited number of ways.

Interpretations must come with interpreters. What are those in the context of the noosphere? All conscious entities, actors, neither, both, or in-betweens are free to interact with the ideas in the noosphere all they want. Think of conscious entities as little spiderlings travelling through our little tapestry of thought. Now, I misled you all a bit, since you don't need to be conscious to interact with and interpret the noosphere. All one needs is to be able to think of ideas, or imagine concepts. The more concepts you can imagine, the more you're proficiently you're able to travel through the noosphere and understand it. A bug will see less nuance to ideas than a human will, of course. The noosphere is so vast it is beyond the simplest definition of infinity, because it includes everything that will ever be and therefor transcends it. There will never be an entity capable of interpreting it in its entirety (with five exceptions).

All conscious things (not just thinking) have an ego. In this case, it isn't my ability to acknowledge how awesome I am. I'm referring to the distinguishment between the 'self' and the 'other.' You are not me, I am not you. An ego is the ability to make the difference. An ego is a gradient, similar to concepts. There is no clearly defined you, only getting closer to what "you" are. Some things do not possess an ego, because they are not conscious. A calculator can think, it can travel through ideas and answers to questions it is prompted to answer, but it cannot distinguish itself from other things. You can do the same things a calculator does, but you know who you are and what you aren't. An ego is a function of a soul. Without an ego, the soul becomes nothing but information. The last thing a soul wants to be reduced to is information. A soul is not an idea in the traditional sense. It is something beyond that.

I would like to reinforce a distinction between thinker and soul. A thinker is fundamentally nothing more than an idea, and it is just information. A calculator. It can move through the noosphere, and certain concepts become part of it when it thinks of them. A calculator answering 48 to a question will have 48 be a part of it. Thinkers are simply information packets that can change which ideas they include and exclude with more freedom than others. A soul is different. At the center of an ego is a soul, but unlike an ego a soul is not an idea. It is not "on" the surface level noosphere. It exists beyond it. It can move through it. Instead of absorbing concepts when it thinks of them, it interprets them (truly). A soul can acknowledge the existence of concepts and their gradients while not overlapping with said concepts via the ego.

Individual interpretations are one thing, but a confluence of all of them forms entirely different phenomena. All thinking things (surface level noospheric actors) operate as one group designated the conscious complex. There is a good reason why it is the conscious complex, because it is the big one with everything in it. You can classify groups of souls and egos into other conscious complexes, but the big one is the conscious complex. A conscious complex acts similarly to an individual conscious, and can make interpretations in much the same way. The imagery of the noosphere being a "web" comes mainly from the conscious complex's interpretation of it. Connections between ideas that are easier for thinkers to think of make for stronger webs, and connections are travelled more often by rule of least resistance.

You've probably caught onto the fact that there is a little more juice in this orange. "Surface level noospheric actors," whatever that means. At the end of the day, the five blind men are still feeling the elephant, and a web always has a weaver (it is not the conscious complex this time).

Most typically we think of reality as a cold, mechanical thing. Not accurate here. We're going to move our discussion to the objective noosphere, and the living reality.

The noosphere has operators that dictate what is objective and what will become reality. Let me introduce you to the subconscious complex. This thing operates on a scope beyond anything in the typical noosphere. Have you ever heard of how there are no original ideas? That's because the subconscious complex has already made it. If you think of the web of ideas sitting in a physical space (noosphere) for a second, you can imagine ideas as an object in that space, but the subconscious complex as the space itself (or at least it creates that space). It is the vessel in which ideas, or anything, can exist. Depending on what the subconscious complex is, ideas, reality, the laws of physics, all of it will tend towards what it is allowed to do within the space.

The subconscious complex can be split up into various miniature subconscious processes which all work in tandem. They are labeled A-F, but have no distinct "order," with the exception of subconscious complex E which is widely considered the "last" or "ultimate" subconscious complex.

Subconscious complex C (conception) is also referred to as the preconscious complex. The preconscious complex is responsible for the formation of ideas; not the subjective interpretation of how to name the web, but the web itself. All concepts (with the sole exception of the soul) are created by the preconscious complex. Any ideas that must be thought of, manifested, or used as reference by either the conscious complex or the subconscious complexes will be first conceived by subconscious complex C. How does the preconscious complex know how to make an idea? The noosphere originally begins as pure information. Pure information is meaningless. Subconscious complex C forces this pure, raw information to consolidate into an idea. Something with meaning, and something that can be interpreted. Subconscious complex C forms the ideas, the conscious complex interprets them and weaves the web, and that's how our little story goes.

Subconscious complex D (destiny) is often thought of as an antagonist to subconscious complex E, but we'll talk about that misconception later. Subconscious complex D is responsible for the principle of action, and can be thought of as a voice in the back of everything's head (and I do mean everything) telling where it should go. Subconscious complex D decides many things, and if you are familiar with the principle of action, you should know it does these things to spin a narrative. Which ideas are easier to think of is controlled by subconscious complex D, which decisions a soul makes are guided by subconscious complex D, how random chance decides to play out is also determined by subconscious complex D.

Subconscious complex B (bosom) is the subconscious complex responsible for the creation of the soul, and the expression of the ego. Similarly to the subconscious complex, a soul is not purely information, and it is not just an idea. However, a soul is not a subconscious complex, it is a conscious thing. It acts with a free will (though that will is guided by the subconscious complex D), and has the capability to distinguish itself from everything else in the noosphere. Subconscious complex B allows these souls to have their unique traits, and is the law of reality that distinguishes a soul from an idea via an ego. Souls are made, like ideas, from pure information, but they are changed via an emergent property that allows them to transcend being simply organized information. These souls' egos will also usually define the soul more clearly by attaching to it to a physical structure or vessel, which is a predefined idea in the noosphere.

Subconscious complex F (firmament) is the gateway between the noosphere and the physical realm. Mind is before matter here, at least ideas are anyway. Subconscious complex F allows ideas to take physical form. For example, at cat can be a cat in real life because there is a concept of a cat in the noosphere, and subconscious complex F manifests that concept into physical reality.

Subconscious complex E (entropy) is a self fulfilling prophecy. It is the most ancient, influential subconscious complex, though you cannot scale them in a traditional sense. Subconscious complex E is also known as the postconscious complex, and it is the inevitable inclination towards disorder (also considered death). When nobody thinks of an idea any longer, it dies, and it is reduced back to a purely informational state by the subconscious complex E. Time too passes in the noosphere, and with time comes entropy, which subconscious complex E ensures. It can do that because it just controls time. An ego is a direct antagonist to subconscious complex E. It is a form of order. Egos however, are nowhere near as powerful as the postconscious complex. With time, they too will fade, and the soul will be reduced down into information.

Let's just clear some stuff up before we're done. The subconscious complex forms the singularity (yes, the physical and the ideological one). It exerts its power through that singularity, where it is based. While it may take more than an eternity, all ideas, souls, and subconscious complexes will die. When subconscious complex E reduces everything else down to pure information, with no more ideas or ability to act on information, the subconscious complexes will die. Subconscious complexes do not have egos, but they do have something akin to a soul. Reality has no need to distinguish itself from anything else, as everything and nothing are a part of itself already. When a soul has its ego whittled away into dust, it will become a part of reality as a piece of pure information. A soul cannot become a subconscious complex this way, as a soul will not be able to exert any will without its ego unlike a subconscious complex; they are fundamentally different. The rules dictate so.

So that was your complete, comprehensive guide to the noosphere. Good luck understanding any of it!
