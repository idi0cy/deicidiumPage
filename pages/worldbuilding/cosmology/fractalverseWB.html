FRACTALVERSE
"Unbounded in our meaningless, unchained to our purpose"(1)

If you've read the noosphere(2), you should know that that plane manifests physically. If you read universes(3), you should know that they are contained in a wider structure. That is the "fractalverse". The fractalverse is a physical structure, which is comprised of four main components: Universes, the fractal membrane, the vacuum, the abyss-void conjugate, and the singularity.

Similarly to a universe(3), the fractalverse has a well defined border that is the fractal membrane. This is designated the fractal membrane, for obvious reasons. While the universal membrane(3a) is relatively easy to breach, as it can exhibit a form of binding exocytosis, the fractal membrane is nearly impossible to violate. Occupying and filling fifteen dimensions(4), exiting the fractal membrane is a logistical nightmare. That isn't to say it can't be done, only that it is nearly impossible. The fractal membrane fills eleven spatial dimensions, and four temporal dimensions(4).

The fractal membrane is a manifestation of subconscious complex F(2a). Because of that, the patterns associated with subconscious complex F, as well as aspects of the universe membrane motif(3a) are an intrinsic part of the fractal membrane. Resolving and mapping information/data collected from the fractal membrane provides the second closest look at any subconscious complex(2a) in the entire fractalverse.

The fractalverse's physical form resembles that of a core with fourteen branches projecting radially. From the center of the structure, eight arms protrude diagonally across each spatial quadrant, while six arms protrude down the three spatial axes. Those are only the main branches of the fractalverse. Mysteriously, tests show that the fractalverse's physical content only naturally extends to four dimensions (3S, 1T), despite the membranes occupying fifteen dimensions(4). It is theorized that this is a direct effect of subconscious complex D's influence on reality. Major branches are named in a systematic order. Axial branches are named either X, Y, or Z, and a negative or positive is added before it to designate orientation. Quadrant branches are sets of three positive or negative values, the first one determining which side on the X axis the quadrant is on, and the second and third do the same for Y and Z (for example, branch ++- is the quadrant with positive x, positive y, and negative z.) This classification system has been made standard across most civilizations(5).

The fractalverse also contains universes within it. As discussed earlier, most universes are a collection of a couple dozen to a hundred solar systems bundled together by a universe membrane. In the fractalverse, roughly one in twenty five units of space are within a universe, or within the membrane of one. This data excludes special universes (to be explained later). Universes are unable to maintain stability outside of the fractalverse. Nothing is able to maintain stability outside of the fractalverse as that region is no longer subject to the influence of subconscious complex F.

Universes are significantly different in physiology and form in two distinct scenarios. Both of these lead us to discuss an important aspect about the fractalverse. Firstly, the fractalverse branches. There are 14 main branches, however there are also sub-branches. Randomly, along each of the fractalverse branches will be a protrusion that another branch extends into. The fractal membrane will angle off and go in a different direction, leaving an equally sized split in the main branch. This split off will have the exact same size and proportions as the main thing.

All branches of the fractal verse extend infinitely far down. There is no limit to how much split of branches there are. Naturally, every cartographer worth their cosmos is asking the same question; why do none of these branches hit each other? Not a single split off branch makes contact with any other branch other than its progenitor. A sub-branch will never it another main branch, or another sub-branch off another sub-branch. The only branch it will ever intersect with is its progenitor. This is an issue, since all the branches are of equal size (around 25,000 light years or so), and they extend infinitely, so it is geographically impossible for this to happen.

For imbeciles, anyway. It's not like the laws of physics and space have to remain constant, and since subconscious complex F is so vastly complex, it can do things like this. It turns out, that the only way the infinite geography of the fractalverse makes sense is if you model it as what it is; a fractal. By applying a space shrinking factor to each map, where sub branches approach infinity in a limited space and main branches do the same, you get a map of the fractalverse. It might take some tweaking, but it will all geographically make sense when mapped as a fractal.

Despite the geography getting all weird and wonky when you take into account other fractal branches, everything inside the fractalverse seems perfectly normal. You will not notice a single geodesic abnormality, a space that does things it shouldn't. It all seems like a regular three dimensional space. It's when you look at the forest that you need to reforge your understanding. Or regrow it? Replant? Whatever.

Back to that universe thing. The first abnormal universe is found at the point in which a fractal branch splits off. Instead of being the normal size, these universes match the exact width of the branch, a gigantic 25,000 light years (we don't really use that measurement but whatever) in diameter. Thankfully, the speed of light isn't as pathetic as it is in our world, or nobody would get anywhere. These branch-off universes also have a six dimensional membrane, adding an extra spatial dimension to it. This makes branch-off universes especially hard to trespass. Branch-off universe membranes actually merge with the fractal membrane, effectively sealing off the branch. This merging of membranes is not possible by traditional dimensional science, especially the merging of the temporal dimensions. The escalation in complexity is something only processable by a subconscious complex.

The final universe type that is different is the most important, interesting structure in all the fractalverse. There is only one of this universe type, and that is because it sits at the helm of the fractalverse. In the center, where all fourteen main branches merge, is the center of the fractalverse. This region is encapsulated by an enormous universe 50 billion light years in diameter. Not only is this the largest universe, it is also surrounded by a variant of fractal membrane instead of universal. It is considered closer to the fractal membrane due to its eleven spatial dimensions, but it has only three temporal dimensions. The subconscious complex F motif (observable imprint of subconscious complex F on reality) is the strongest here. At the very center of this universe is the singularity.

The singularity is at the very center of the fractalverse, and it is a sphere only about the size of a small galaxy. Since the singularity is the core of all the subconscious complexes, the closer you get to it the more you feel their impact. Subconscious complexes A, B, and D have some of the most noticeable effects the closer you get to it. Subconscious complex D has its regular effect, and subconscious complexes... well, I shouldn't say. But other than those, the strongest subconscious complex here is E. Subconscious complex F actually has a much weaker effect, as it is an antagonist to subconscious complex E, which always seems to win no matter what. Subconscious complex D works with E, while B can only resist E... well, I shouldn't say.

So what's outside of the fractalverse? Universal membranes have outsides, so what's beyond this one? Well, unfortunately, nothing good. The fractal membrane is subconscious complex F's influence's last stand against subconscious complex E's. Past that, it's influence degrades exponentially, and following its disappearance are subconscious complexes D, B, A, and then C (in order). Reality doesn't work here. You can try to travel to other branches or float around, it won't work. This outside space is called the abyss, and it is only deemed that because you can go back if you don't float too far beyond the influence of subconscious complex F. Past that though, is the void. There's no coming back from that one.

Luckily, the fractalverse is practically impossible to degrade unlike a universe, so there is a guaranteed chance that it'll stick around. Every subconscious complex except for maybe C and E have a vested interest in keeping it around, and while they probably couldn't overpower subconscious complex E even when combined, it's nice enough to have some patience.
