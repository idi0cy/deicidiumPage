Title: Much Work Ahead

Author: Hashoel Mayn

Date: COME UP WITH IT EVENTUALLY

Description: Recorded recollection of an inquiry I believe holds great meaning. Likely the first of many.

I'm not a proffesional scalper. Or I guess scavenger would be a more accurate term for what I've brought in today. More of a hobbyist. That's probably going to change after today.

I'm not a vocal revolutionary, but the information I have on me is too valuable not to document. There's absolutely no chance I can let any of this data I have get either destroyed, or placed back into the hands of the Presiding Authoritate. I'm using all of the technical knowledge that I can to make sure this will never be lost.

These logs are intended for anybody to read, so I must provide the full context. I'm currently living in Talrick. It's a peripheral organ of Infinitrium in the --+ quadrant. Originally the oppresor's presence was strong, but they've since withered as result of the conflict between this province and Amentic Inc. I won't pretend to have a branch eyed view of any of that, but what I do know is that the denizens of this land have suffered.

Infinitrium is slowly backing out of my home world, and their influence weakens by the day.

We're all finally closer to freedom; be it freedom from oppression, or the freedom of death.

This isn't about that. This is about the drive. Constant shellings on critical pieces of infrastructure have left buildings decimated, and given my recent freedom I took an action which upon looking back resembles a flight clipping near the sun. Zone 55 was rumored to be assumed by the government. Of course, that's every zone here. But this one was thought to be truly a part of it.

I now have reason to believe it was counterintelligence. Carving into the viscera of what was once a highly secured server room, I took possession of the drive.

The drive isn't large. Small enough to fit comfortably in the palm of my hand. More concerningly, I discovered it lying in the shattered vault. The government possesses many such vaults. Counterintelligence vaults are of greatest concern.

Most strikingly, the design is not of any native origin. This is entirely different. It resembles the technology I've become so tragically familiar with native to Amentic inc. I don't know what their drive is doing under Infinitrium command.

I will begin decoding this drive tomorrow. It's already caused me anxiety and paranoia from purely ownership, a feeling not entirely alien to me.

If there is a single thing I can take away from this war before it throws me to the latter freedom, it will be whatever is in that drive.

[END LOG]
